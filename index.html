About Ocean Network

A global community working to keep oceans healthy, resilient and productive for future generations.

Our Mission

We mobilize communities, scientists and policymakers to reduce pollution, restore habitats, and promote sustainable fisheries and shipping.

Since 2012 we've coordinated thousands of coastal cleanups, supported marine protected areas, and educated millions about ocean stewardship.
